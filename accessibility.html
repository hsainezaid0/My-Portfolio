BEHIND THE PORTFOLIO

Accessibility

Making this portfolio easier to explore, for everyone.

Accessibility status

The aim is to make this portfolio usable by as many people as possible. A comprehensive accessibility audit has not been completed, so no WCAG or RGAA conformance level is claimed.

Using the site

  • Use Tab and Shift + Tab to move between interactive elements, and Enter to activate a link.
  • On small screens, use the Menu button to open navigation. Escape closes the menu.
  • Enable your device's reduced-motion preference to stop the particle animation and reduce decorative effects.
  • Use your browser's zoom controls to enlarge the page.

The information pages include a “Skip to content” link at the start of the page.

What still needs evaluation?

Full keyboard navigation, screen-reader behaviour, colour contrast across all states, and usability at high zoom still need a complete manual review. External websites linked from this portfolio have their own accessibility arrangements.

Report a barrier

If a page is difficult to use, please reach out through the contact page or open a GitHub issue. Include the page address, what you were trying to do, and your browser or assistive technology if you wish. Avoid posting private information in public issues.
